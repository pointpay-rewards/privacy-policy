Privacy Policy

Clish Rewards ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Email address provided during registration
  • Unique User Identifier (UID)
  • App progress and accumulated virtual coins
  • Reward and withdrawal history
  • Advertisement interaction data
  • Device information (device type, OS version, app version)
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device identifier (Device ID used for security and anti-fraud purposes)
  • Payment information submitted for withdrawal purposes

In order to ensure the proper functionality and security of the application, Clish Rewards may collect a unique device identifier. This identifier helps us recognize the device being used to access the app.

The device identifier is used strictly for operational purposes such as preventing abuse of the reward system, detecting fraudulent activity, ensuring fair use of the platform, and maintaining the stability of the service.

Collecting a device identifier also helps us avoid multiple account abuse, bot activity, emulator usage, and other behaviors that may compromise the integrity of the rewards system.

The device identifier does not grant us access to personal files, contacts, photos, messages, or any private content stored on the user's device.

2. Payment Information

When users reach the minimum withdrawal threshold and request a payout, they may be required to provide payment details. This may include:

  • PayPal email address
  • Digital wallet information
  • Other payment identifiers required for processing rewards

Payment information is used solely for the purpose of processing withdrawals and preventing fraud. We do not sell or use this information for marketing purposes.

Payment data may be processed by third-party payment service providers. We do not store full financial credentials such as passwords or complete banking authentication data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Account creation and authentication
  • Tracking coins and rewards
  • Processing withdrawal requests
  • Fraud detection and abuse prevention
  • Providing and optimizing advertisements
  • Compliance with legal obligations

4. Advertising Services

The App provides rewarded advertisements. Users receive virtual coins in exchange for valid ad views. Advertising partners may collect device identifiers and interaction data for analytics and fraud prevention.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only with:

  • Authentication and database service providers
  • Advertising partners
  • Payment processing providers
  • Legal authorities when required

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as necessary to operate the App and fulfill reward obligations, or as required by law. Users may request account deletion at any time.

7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user information. However, no system is completely secure. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for individuals under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of changes.

10. Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact: suporteneorestudio@gmail.com

11. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Access their personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of their account and data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
